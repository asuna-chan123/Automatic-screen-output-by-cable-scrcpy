## 🌟 Overview

**MirrorFlux Pro** is a powerful, lightweight, and modern Windows GUI for the legendary [scrcpy](https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy). It transforms a complex command-line tool into a professional dashboard, making Android mirroring accessible to everyone—from gamers to developers.

## ✨ Premium Features

### 🔌 Intelligent Connectivity
- **Auto-Discovery:** Instant detection of USB-connected devices using Windows Management Instrumentation (WMI).
- **Wireless Freedom:** Seamlessly connect via WiFi (IP & Port). Supports manual entry or **One-Click Auto WiFi Setup**.
- **Smart Status:** Real-time monitoring of device states (Ready, Connected, Offline, Unauthorized).

### 📋 Device Management
- **Persistent Custom Naming:** Assign friendly aliases to your devices (e.g., "Main Phone", "Test Tablet") that stay saved even after a restart.
- **Search & Filter:** Instantly find your device using the real-time search bar.
- **Advanced Sorting:** Organize your dashboard by Name (A-Z), WiFi devices first, or Cable devices first.
- **Clean UI:** Delete old or unwanted device entries with a single click.

### ⚙️ Power Features
- **Screen-Off Mirroring:** Toggle the `--turn-screen-off` flag with a sleek, modern switch to save battery and prevent burn-in.
- **Automatic Environment Detection:** Smartly locates your `scrcpy` and `adb` binaries regardless of where the app is launched.
- **Responsive Design:** A fluid interface that adapts as you resize the window.

## 🛠️ Getting Started

### 📦 Installation
1. Download the latest **MirrorFlux Pro** release.
2. Place the `ScrcpyMonitor.exe` in your `scrcpy` folder (where `adb.exe` and `scrcpy.exe` reside).
3. Run and enjoy!

### 📶 WiFi Setup (The Easy Way)
1. Connect your phone via USB cable first.
2. Click **"Auto WiFi"** on your device card in the dashboard.
3. Once the result pops up, you can unplug the cable and click **"Connect"** to go wireless!
